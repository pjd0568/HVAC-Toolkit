"h/yr" next to each Summer/Winter percentage is the number of hours a typical year spends at or beyond that condition, not a rare "1-in-N-year" event — it recurs to that degree most years. The Return Period Extremes column IS a return-period estimate (ASHRAE HOF Ch.14 extreme-annual / Gumbel method): the mean and standard deviation of each year's extreme, extrapolated to the 5/10/20/50-year value. Its dry-bulb and wet-bulb figures are independent extremes — the hottest and the most-humid hour of a year are usually different hours — so no combined RH/dew-point is shown for them. The single absolute record max/min (and when it occurred) is in Extended data below.
