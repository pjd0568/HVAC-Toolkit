Data provenance. Any future in-app lookup derived from ERA5 reanalysis (Open-Meteo) is not a licensed ASHRAE/CIBSE station table — intended for preliminary design; verify against published tables for critical projects. Reanalysis tends to damp extremes relative to station observations (cooling dry-bulb often ~0.5–2 °C lower in our spot-checks — see Methodology).
