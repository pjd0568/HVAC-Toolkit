These bands drive the traffic-light dots on the Pipe Sizing catalogue. They are a lifecycle-cost framing for closed LTHW and CHW distribution — not a substitute for project acoustic, erosion or manufacturer limits. Friction itself is still calculated with Darcy–Weisbach / Colebrook–White (CIBSE Guide C).

Green = ideal mid-band · Amber = lower/upper limit bands or the transition between them · Red = outside the acceptable range.

Pressure gradient (ΔP/L)

ScenarioRangeNotes
Lower limit100 – 150 Pa/mCAPEX-heavy / OPEX-light — oversized pipework, low pumping energy. Headers and low-loss distribution.
Ideal design200 – 300 Pa/mIndustry sweet spot — balanced lifecycle cost between pipe cost and pumping energy.
Upper limit400 – 500 Pa/mCAPEX-light / OPEX-heavy — smaller pipe, higher pump head, energy and acoustic risk.

Below 100 Pa/m or above 500 Pa/m is outside limits (red). The amber bands cover 100–200 Pa/m (below ideal) and 300–500 Pa/m (above ideal but still within the upper commercial range).

Recommended size

The Pipe Sizing tool highlights the smallest DN with ideal friction (200–300 Pa/m) and velocity that is not red — a starting point, not an automatic selection. Always check noise, available space and any manufacturer constraints for the job.
