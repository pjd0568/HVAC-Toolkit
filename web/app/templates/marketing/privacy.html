Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026 · Controller: {{ legal_entity }}

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ legal_entity }} (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use {{ brand_name }} (the “Service”).

We process personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. For privacy requests, contact {{ contact_email }}.

1. Who we are

The data controller is {{ legal_entity }}. The Service is offered at hvac-calcs.app. Contact: {{ contact_email }}.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — email address, password hash, account and subscription status, trial dates.
  • Usage data — pages and tools you use, unit preference (SI/IP), session identifiers, approximate technical logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) needed to secure and operate the Service.
  • Calculation inputs — values you enter into tools (for example temperatures, flows, dimensions). These are processed to produce results; we do not treat them as a customer CRM database.
  • Support messages — name, email, subject category, and message content when you contact us.
  • Billing data — when payments are enabled, our payment provider processes card and invoice details. We typically receive limited billing metadata (for example customer id, plan, payment status), not full card numbers.

3. Why we use data (purposes and legal bases)

  • Provide the Service (contract) — create and authenticate accounts, run calculators, remember preferences during your session, apply trial and subscription access, and send essential account emails (trial start, subscription confirmation, cancellation).
  • Support and operations (legitimate interests / contract) — respond to contact forms, fix bugs, prevent abuse, maintain security.
  • Billing (contract / legal obligation) — process subscriptions and keep tax/accounting records when payments are live.
  • Improve the product (legitimate interests) — understand which tools are used and improve reliability and documentation. We avoid using your project inputs for unrelated marketing.
  • Legal compliance (legal obligation) — respond to lawful requests and keep required records.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and similar storage for signed-in sessions and security. These are required for the Service to work. We do not currently use advertising cookies. If we add analytics cookies later, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain consent.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run the Service (for example cloud hosting and database);
  • Email providers used to receive or send support and transactional messages;
  • Payment processors when subscriptions are enabled;
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.

Processors act on our instructions and under appropriate agreements where required.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the UK, EEA, or other countries. Where data leaves the UK/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum or adequacy decisions) as applicable.

7. Retention

  • Account data — while your account is active, then a short period for backup and dispute handling, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Support emails — typically up to 24 months, unless needed longer for an ongoing matter.
  • Billing records — as required for tax and accounting (often six years in the UK).
  • Security logs — for a limited period appropriate to detecting abuse.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures (including TLS in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls). No online service is completely secure; please use a strong unique password.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • request erasure in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • data portability where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

To exercise these rights, email {{ contact_email }} with subject [Privacy]. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

10. Children

The Service is intended for professional adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Significant changes may also be notified on the site or by email.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: {{ contact_email }} · Contact form · Related: Terms of Service
