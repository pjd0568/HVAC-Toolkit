Calculation methods

This page records how the current calculators are derived. It is a description of the software, not a certificate of compliance with ASHRAE, CIBSE or any other body.

Psychrometrics and AHU processes

Moist-air properties follow ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals Chapter 1 relations (saturation pressure, humidity ratio, enthalpy, wet-bulb).

Assumptions. Cooling-coil results use the on-coil and off-coil states you enter. A wet coil is often taken near 90–95% RH leaving; that is a design assumption, not a solved coil geometry. AHU design chooses the process from the moisture balance (cool/dehumidify/reheat, heat then humidify, or sensible-only). Outdoor air is entered as a volume flow; the fresh-air fraction is derived from dry-air mass.

Units. The engine is SI. The SI/IP control converts the values you see and type.

Limits. These tools do not size coil rows, face velocity or condensate drainage.

Duct sizing

Friction uses Darcy–Weisbach with the Swamee–Jain explicit Colebrook–White equation. Rectangular equivalent diameter uses Huebscher (ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch. 21). Flat oval uses Heyt & Diaz (1975): De = 1.55·A0.625/P0.25.

Limits. Index-leg fitting K-values and the ×1.5 flexible-duct friction factor are indicative planning figures, not the full geometry-dependent tables in ASHRAE Chapter 32 or CIBSE Guide C. Check critical runs against manufacturer or primary data. The free page sizes one section. The index run is a Pro workflow.

Hydronic pipe

Friction uses Darcy–Weisbach / Colebrook–White, in the form used with CIBSE Guide C. Catalogue dimensions follow BS EN 10255, BS EN 1057 and DIN 11850.

Water properties follow published CIBSE/ASHRAE tables. Ethylene and propylene glycol use ASHRAE secondary-coolant correlations. Hydratech Coolflow DTX, IGE and NTP use the manufacturer’s published data sheets.

Limits. Velocity bands are per nominal size (DN 50 and below capped at 1.5 m/s, in line with the intent of CIBSE Guide C Table 2.2). A pressure gradient around 200–300 Pa/m is a lifecycle-cost framing, not a named code limit. Fitting K-values are indicative. The free page is one pipe. Pump head for a circuit is the Pro system workflow.

Refrigeration

Saturation and single-phase properties come from CoolProp (HEOS). The PT table reports bubble (quality 0) and dew (quality 1) pressures as barg relative to 1.01325 bar absolute. Zeotropic blends show glide.

Evaporating temperature is paired with dew pressure. Condensing temperature is paired with bubble pressure. Mass flow for line sizing is capacity divided by evaporator enthalpy change.

ACR copper sizes follow BS EN 12735-1. The DX sizer checks velocity bands (suction about 8–15 m/s, liquid about 0.5–1.5 m/s, discharge about 10–18 m/s) and warns on low suction velocity for oil return. It does not yet check equivalent saturation-temperature pressure drop. Transcritical R-744 is out of scope.

Superheat and subcooling. SH = Tsuction − Tdew(Plow). SC = Tbubble(Phigh) − Tliquid. Colour bands are typical field guidance, not a manufacturer target. This is not a charge calculation. The charge calculator is not available.

Weather

In-app weather and design-condition lookup is not available. Where a future lookup uses reanalysis, it will be a grid average, not a licensed ASHRAE or CIBSE station table, and it will be labelled as such.
