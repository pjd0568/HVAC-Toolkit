Methods and limits

Moist air follows ASHRAE Fundamentals-style relations. Duct friction is Darcy–Weisbach. Pipe friction follows the same family of equations with catalogue bores. Refrigerant saturation properties come from CoolProp. Citing a reference is not a claim of certification or code compliance.

Results are a design aid. Verify critical selections against licensed data, manufacturer information and the project specification.
