About

HVAC-CALCS is an independent {{ legal_entity }} product, created around real HVAC design and site workflows. The calculators cover the checks engineers repeat: moist-air state, a duct or pipe section, a refrigerant saturation lookup, and the longer workflows that follow when a single number is not enough.

Methods, assumptions and limits are published so a result can be reviewed before anyone creates an account. A cited standard is a reference for the method, not a certification or a claim that a project complies with that standard.
