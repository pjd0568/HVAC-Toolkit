Methodology & Limitations
Standards basis, disclosed simplifications, and climate-data provenance for the calculation tools. Intended for preliminary / planning design — verify critical projects against licensed tables, manufacturer data, and project-specific limits.
Psychrometrics & AHU

Moist-air properties follow ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals Chapter 1 relations (saturation pressure, humidity ratio, enthalpy, wet-bulb).

Cooling Coil is defined by the on- and off-coil states you enter. A wet (dehumidifying) coil typically leaves air at ~90–95% RH; that is a design assumption, not a solved coil geometry.

AHU Design (supply-driven) derives off-coil from the required supply moisture at a target off-coil RH (default 90%, matching typical wet-coil practice and the Cooling Coil tool). Set 100% RH for the idealised contact-factor = 1 case (saturated at the supply dew point). The two tools are comparable when they use the same off-coil RH idealisation.

Duct sizing

Friction via Darcy–Weisbach with Swamee–Jain explicit Colebrook–White. Rectangular equivalent diameter: Huebscher (ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch.21). Flat oval: Heyt & Diaz (1975) / ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch.21 — De = 1.55·A0.625/P0.25.

Index-leg fitting K-values and the ×1.5 flex-duct friction derate are indicative planning figures, not full ASHRAE Ch.32 / CIBSE Guide C geometry-dependent tables. Verify critical or high-pressure runs against manufacturer or primary data.

Pipe sizing

Friction via Darcy–Weisbach / Colebrook–White (CIBSE Guide C). Pipe dimensions from BS EN 10255, BS EN 1057, DIN 11850.

Velocity traffic lights use per-DN bands (DN ≤ 50 max 1.5 m/s aligns with CIBSE Guide C Table 2.2; larger DN allow higher velocities). ΔP/L bands (ideal 200–300 Pa/m) are a lifecycle-cost framing, not a named code table. See Pipe Sizing Design Limits.

Fitting/valve K-values are indicative planning figures — not full CIBSE/Crane geometry tables.

Climate / design weather

Percentile method follows ASHRAE HOF Ch.14 (per-year then average). When reanalysis lookup is enabled, design conditions are derived from Open-Meteo ERA5 / ERA5-Land — not licensed ASHRAE or CIBSE station tables.

Reanalysis is a grid-cell average and tends to damp extremes versus station observations. Spot-checks against NCEI Engineering Weather Data (station percentiles, 1988–2017) showed cooling 0.4% dry-bulb typically ~0.5–1.8 °C lower for UK/EU sites; heating 99.6% often milder by ~0.5–2.5 °C. Use published ASHRAE/CIBSE tables for critical plant sizing.
