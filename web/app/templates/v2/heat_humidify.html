Heating & humidification notes

Two-stage winter path. A heating coil raises dry-bulb at constant humidity ratio (1 → X). Steam humidification then adds moisture at constant dry-bulb (X → 2). That is the standard AHU sequence when both heat and humidity are needed.

Duties. Sensible load is the moist-air enthalpy rise across the heating coil. Latent load is the isothermal steam enthalpy rise (≈ ṁw · hfg with hfg ≈ 2501 kJ/kg).

Mode A — state target. Enter states 1 and 2; the tool forms intermediate X at (T2, W1) and reports coil / steam duties.

Mode B — duty & airflow. Enter heating kW and steam rate; leaving T and W are calculated, with X at (T2, W1).

Further reading. See the reference article Thermodynamics of Steam Humidification for the energy balance behind the isothermal chart model and when a full enthalpy-slope solution is warranted.
