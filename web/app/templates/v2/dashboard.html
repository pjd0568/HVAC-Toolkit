Add to Home Screen

Install this toolkit like an app on your phone — full screen, one tap from your Home Screen.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome or an in-app browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ (top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm with Install or Add.

After installing, open from the Home Screen icon for the full-screen app experience. More detail is in Help.
