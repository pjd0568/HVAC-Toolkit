Welcome

HVAC-calcs is a browser-based toolkit for day-to-day HVAC design and site work — psychrometrics, air and water distribution, refrigeration diagnostics, and reference utilities. Calculations run from clear engineering methods (see Methodology); SI and imperial units switch anywhere in the app.

Suites keep each tool’s inputs in this browser tab so you can jump between related calculators without starting again. Add the site to your phone home screen for a quick site-day launcher.

More modules are on the roadmap — room loads, grille throw, expansion vessels, buffer tanks, fresh-air / CO₂ modelling, acoustics, and further refrigeration tools. If something is missing for your workflow, request it on the Contact us page.
