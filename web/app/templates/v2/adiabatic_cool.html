Adiabatic cooling notes

Process. Direct evaporative / spray washers with no external heat transfer follow the constant wet-bulb line (nearly constant enthalpy). Dry-bulb falls and humidity rises toward the entering wet-bulb temperature.

Effectiveness. ε = (Tdb1 − Tdb2) / (Tdb1 − Twb1). Rigid media typically land around 80–90%; ε = 100% is the thermodynamic limit.

Mode A. Set media effectiveness or a leaving RH on the wet-bulb line; the tool reports leaving state, cooling effect and water use.

Mode B. Set the required sensible cooling capacity; leaving dry-bulb and required ε are calculated. Duties that would push below the entering wet-bulb are rejected.
