Desiccant application notes

Process. Desiccant dehumidification follows (or sits slightly above) a constant-enthalpy line toward hotter, drier air. Latent heat released by adsorption/absorption appears as a sensible temperature rise.

Ideal vs real. ηd = 100% holds h2 = h1. Lower efficiency adds heat of adsorption: Δh = (1 − ηd) · hfg · (W1 − W2), with hfg = 2501 kJ/kg.

Mode A — state target. Set entering air and a leaving humidity ratio or RH; the tool solves leaving dry-bulb on the process line.

Mode B — duty & airflow. Set entering air, airflow and moisture removal rate; leaving W follows from the dry-air mass balance, then dry-bulb from the enthalpy model.

After the wheel. Leaving air is typically too warm for supply — a cooling coil or heat exchanger downstream is usually required to trim dry-bulb without undoing the dehumidification.
